Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying verified plots in Rajasthan

How do I schedule a site visit to the plots?

+
You can easily schedule a complimentary site visit by filling out the form on this page or clicking our WhatsApp button. Our local project manager will arrange door-to-door transportation and take you on a detailed guided tour of the township and surrounding infrastructure.

Are all plots 100% government approved (JDA / UIT / ADA / RERA)?

+
Yes. Every project listed through RajasthanPlot has clear government approvals (JDA in Jaipur, ADA in Ajmer, UIT in Bhilwara, KDA in Kota) along with RERA registration numbers. All titles are verified by property advocates to guarantee zero disputes.

How does the bank loan facility work?

+
Because our townships and plots are 100% government approved, leading nationalized and private banks (including State Bank of India, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Bank of Baroda) offer up to 75%–80% financing on our land plots. Our dedicated loan desk handles all paperwork for you.

Can I receive plot maps and rate lists directly on WhatsApp?

+
Absolutely! Just click on the “Chat via WhatsApp” button or message our team directly. We will instantly send the master layout plan, available plot numbers, facing directions, DLC rates, and pricing calculator directly to your phone.

What documents are required for registry and ownership transfer?

+
For Indian residents, you simply need your Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and passport-size photographs. For NRIs, passport copies and NRE/NRO banking credentials are required. Our legal legal desk takes care of deed drafting, stamp duty calculation, and Sub-Registrar appointment.
